Hydration (what's that?)

What it means
Hydration is how much water is in your dough compared to the flour, as a percent. Flour always counts as 100%.

Formula: water ÷ flour × 100
Example: 350 g water ÷ 500 g flour = 0.70, so 70% hydration.

Counting the starter: starter is half flour and half water (a 100% starter). Add half of it to the flour and half to the water, then do the same math. It nudges the number up a little.

What the numbers feel like:
55-65%: stiff, easy to handle
68-72%: great for beginners
75-80%: sticky, bigger holes, harder to shape
More water = stickier dough and a more open crumb.
